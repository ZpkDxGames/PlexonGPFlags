# Third-party notices

## GriefPrevention
PlexonGPFlags requires GriefPrevention and integrates through its public Bukkit/Paper API. GriefPrevention is not bundled in the PlexonGPFlags JAR.

Project: https://github.com/GriefPrevention/GriefPrevention

## GriefPreventionEasyGUI
The player-facing claim-management workflow in PlexonGPFlags was informed by the publicly documented feature set of GriefPreventionEasyGUI by hope61 (claim list/info, auto-claim, trust, resize, teleport, abandon and visualizer concepts).

PlexonGPFlags implements its own native Paper inventory/session, teleport and visualizer architecture and does not bundle GriefPreventionEasyGUI, InvUI or bStats code.

Project: https://github.com/hope61/GriefPreventionEasyGUI
